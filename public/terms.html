3. Accuracy

Income limits come from USDA's FY2026 Adjusted Income Limits (HB-1-3550, Appendix 9, PN 657, dated 07/13/2026). We fingerprint the source file and show the page each number came from. USDA can change limits at any time. County types and Opportunity Zone counts are guides, not decisions: property eligibility is confirmed by exact address on USDA's eligibility map, and USDA counts adjusted income, which can differ from what you enter. Always confirm with USDA or your lender.
